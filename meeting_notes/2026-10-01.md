## Iteration: The Two Circles

In relation to the **Virtual Memory Visualizer** project, we discussed a
two-stage approach to creating things.

### First Circle

The first circle is intentionally **crude and rough**.

The objective is to create something that exists and works rather than
attempting to make it perfect.

### Second Circle

The second circle is **refinement**.

Once the rough version exists, improve it and make it better.

A recurring problem is attempting to draw the "perfect circle" on the first
attempt. This can prevent or delay actually producing something.

The principle is:

1. Make the rough circle.
2. Refine the circle.
